# Business framing

## Product thesis

Build a geospatial portfolio decision workspace for Bedashing Beauty’s **Head of Retail / Portfolio and Expansion Lead**. It converts public, imperfect market signals into transparent *strategic hypotheses*: which lounges merit protection, observation, or shrink review; and which urban cells warrant growth research.

This persona owns a network-level capital and coverage trade-off. A COO and executive leadership are secondary consumers. It is not a consumer salon finder or a tool that decides closures.

## Decisions and signals

| Decision | Signals | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Protect / hold / investigate shrink | peer-relative reputation, observable demand proxy, similarity-weighted competition, self-overlap, coverage uniqueness, data confidence | Existing-branch recommendation + evidence |
| Study an expansion area | residential/lifestyle/amenity proxy, competitor validation vs saturation, own-network distance, exclusion/access context | H3 opportunity layer + ranked cells |
| Prioritize field validation | low-confidence labels, stale evidence, conflicting locations, sensitivity | Evidence queue and scenario deltas |

## Market and peer context

Bedashing’s official website calls it a contemporary award-winning premium salon with 24 UAE lounges, founded in 2008 with an Emirati identity and women’s empowerment mission. Its offer spans hair, nails, face, and body/grooming; the proprietary multi-service chair positions it around time-efficient premium pampering. Sources: [official home](https://bedashingbeauty.com/), [Who We Are](https://bedashingbeauty.com/who-we-are/), [How We Do It](https://bedashingbeauty.com/how-we-do-it/), accessed 2026-09-08.

**INFERENCE:** branch archetypes should be captured before comparison: airport/transit, mall/high-footfall, premium destination/urban high street, neighborhood/community, and residential/villa. A high-footfall or transit lounge has different observable demand and catchment assumptions from a residential lounge. Phase 1 must assign only source-supported archetypes; otherwise `unknown`.

## Guardrail

Public ratings, POIs, and geographic coverage are market signals—not revenue, utilization, profitability, customer origin, lease cost, or closure evidence. Labels mean “prioritize strategic review,” not “execute a closure/opening.”
